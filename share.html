2Get your GitHub repo URL

Create a public repo, and copy the link.

  1. Log in to github.com/new.
  2. Give the repo a name, keep it Public (the default settings are fine), and click Create repository.
  3. Copy the repo link from your browser's address bar. It looks like https://github.com/your-name/your-repo.

3Get a live demo URL Encouraged

Optional, but your showcase card gets a Live demo button so people can try your build. Follow the steps below to get your demo URL.

  1. Open your app in AI Studio
  2. Click Publish in the upper-right corner.
  3. Click Get Started, then Publish App.
  4. Copy the URL it gives you. It ends in .run.app or .ai.studio.

Deploy with Firebase Hosting, Cloud Run, or Vercel. The easiest way: ask your agent to do it, or to walk you through the terminal commands.

Deploy this app so anyone can open it in a browser, using Firebase Hosting, Cloud Run, or Vercel (pick whichever fits this app best). Keep API keys out of the client code. Walk me through each terminal command step by step, and give me the public URL at the end.

Your link will look like https://your-app.web.app, https://your-app-xxxx.run.app or https://your-app.vercel.app.

That's fine — leave the field blank. Your repo and screenshot are enough. You can also record a short screen capture (Loom or YouTube) and paste it into Video / walkthrough URL instead.
